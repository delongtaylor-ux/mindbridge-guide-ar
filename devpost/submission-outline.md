## Project name
MindBridge Guide AR

## Proposed tagline
Hands-on Montessori fraction lessons, with spatial coaching for the adult and independence for the learner.

## Proposed pitch
MindBridge Guide AR is designed to help parents and educators demonstrate fractions beside real learning materials. The first module, Fraction Workshop, rehearses building a whole, finding equivalents, and attempting a fresh challenge. The adult chooses when guidance appears and records how much help the learner needed.

## Problem and intended solution
A parent may have concrete learning materials without knowing how to demonstrate the next step. The proposed Lens keeps a short, replayable visual demonstration beside those materials. Its central moment shows two quarters matching a half, then removes the demonstration so the adult can observe independent work.

## Demo storyboard — target 2 minutes 40 seconds

| Time | Show in the actual working Lens | Narration goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:20 | Adult-facing workshop and preview label | Identify the parent/educator audience and hands-on learning problem. |
| 0:20–0:40 | Place the workshop and walk around it | Show why the demonstration belongs beside materials in space. |
| 0:40–1:10 | Replay two quarters filling one half | Make the fraction relationship and interaction clear. |
| 1:10–1:35 | Build a whole; demonstrate a rejected incorrect attempt | Show real behavior and self-correction. |
| 1:35–2:00 | Hide hints; attempt a new challenge | Show guidance receding and honest assistance tracking. |
| 2:00–2:20 | Save and correct a session observation | Explain that the adult records observations; the prototype does not infer mastery. |
| 2:20–2:40 | Brief, actual CLAD workflow, actual test results | Explain what was learned and what remains unproven. |

The video must show the real Lens preview, not a rendered concept or a web substitute. Keep it under three minutes and publicly playable on YouTube.
